Human Resource Management System

Talent development is vital for long-term business success. Unlike traditional Asian talent management, we focus on "Help People Do Better" to offer modular HR solutions.

Vision

Optimizing Enterprise Efficiency through Systematized Human Resources

We provide full-scale solutions, not just tools. Our approach systematizes processes like a HR training company, integrating consultancy techniques. We scientifically handle repetitive tasks from data acquisition to survey analysis. Our flexible, standardized, modular design helps companies improve HR management quickly. We also customize solutions to fit each organization's needs.

Design Guideline

To ensure consistency in style, detailed definitions are needed for layout, colors, tables, components, and icons. The Style Guideline serves not only as a guide for the design team but also facilitates effective communication with other development teams.

Providing Modular Human Resources Development Solutions

Linking Challenges to Performance

The gamification model incentivizes employees to engage with challenging tasks by tying them to performance metrics, ultimately enhancing efficiency and productivity.

Real-time Performance Tracking

Real-time Performance Tracking facilitates easy monitoring of announcements, employee feedback, surveys, and departmental chat groups. It enhances communication and organizational effectiveness.

Customized Learning Journeys

Integrate online and offline training resources, allowing employees to focus on improving job skills and creating customized learning journeys tailored to their needs with permission control.

Organizational Communication

Announcements, Employee Feedback, Survey, and Messaging capabilities grouped by departments support a two-way channel to enhance communication efficiency.

Journey

Designing a stellar onboarding journey for new hires

Journey defines onboarding as a systematic approach to integrating new employees with a company's culture, providing them with the tools and information needed for productivity. A well-designed, engaging onboarding process significantly boosts employee engagement.

Talent Management System

Simplify and streamline complex and tedious routine reports with data science

The remote management platform uses big data and new technologies to help businesses with tasks like employee onboarding, transfers, salary adjustments, retention, departures, training, and performance evaluations, all fully recorded by the system.
