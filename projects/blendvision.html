The Challenge

How to transform videos into accessible knowledge using AI

How to transform videos into knowledge and make that knowledge easily discoverable using AI. This requires efficiently processing and extracting key information from videos while ensuring that the knowledge is searchable and accessible, so users can quickly find the content they need.

Why Was This Project Important?

The AI-powered chat solution was developed in response to the critical need for efficient, accessible, and customizable tools that can cater to the unique requirements of businesses across various sectors. It offers a way to integrate specialized knowledge seamlessly into businesses' operations, enhancing customer support, decision-making, and operational efficiency without the need for extensive IT resources or expertise.

Research Insights

Understanding user pain points and market needs

Working with product owners on researching user journeys and competitive analysis helped to understand user pain points and develop a product strategy. Assistance was provided in visualizing discussion scenarios and user flow, facilitating communication with stakeholders.

Research process — user journey mapping and competitive analysis

Insight 01
Solving Customer Problems with Simple Steps

The design should be easy to use and efficient, allowing users to set up and start using the AI quickly, without needing technical expertise. The main focus should be on making the interface simple, simplifying how data is entered into the AI, and providing clear, easy-to-follow instructions to help users gain valuable insights from the data.

Insight 02
Customization and Integration Drive User Adoption

To make the product popular with users, it needs to be easily customizable and integrate smoothly into current business workflows without causing disruptions. This means ensuring it connects well with tools like Slack, Jira, and Google Drive, and allowing businesses to personalize the AI's responses and actions to match their brand.

Usability Testing & POC

Before launching our product, we conducted usability tests and a Proof of Concept (POC) for the Japanese and Taiwanese market. This validation process was crucial for adapting our product to local preferences, improving usability, and ensuring market viability. It not only reduced the risk of post-launch failure but also provided a competitive edge by aligning the product with user and market expectations.
