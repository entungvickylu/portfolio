01 — Overview

Turning leadership data into growth opportunities

Organizations invest heavily in leadership development programs but often struggle to measure impact or make them actionable at scale. This platform was designed to bridge the gap between HR assessment frameworks and the day-to-day tools managers use.

My role covered the full product design process: from stakeholder interviews and HR framework analysis to information architecture, interaction design, and design system creation.

"HR teams had the data. Managers had no idea what to do with it. The design challenge was translating complex competency models into clear, motivating next steps."

02 — The Challenge

Complex frameworks, human-scale interface

01

Multi-Stakeholder Needs

HR administrators, managers, and individual employees each needed a different view of the same underlying data — with different levels of access and different goals.

02

Complex Data Visualization

Leadership competency scores, 360° feedback, and development progress needed to be presented clearly without overwhelming users or requiring HR expertise to interpret.

03

Driving Action

The platform needed to move users from passive assessment to active development — surfacing recommended actions, learning resources, and progress milestones.

03 — Design Process

Research → Architecture → Iteration

I started by interviewing HR directors and team managers to understand how they currently use leadership data and where decisions break down. Mapping the user journey revealed that most "development" happened in annual review cycles — not ongoing. The product needed to change that cadence.

Research & Wireframe Screenshots

The dashboard architecture centered on three views: individual growth (employee-facing), team health (manager-facing), and organizational overview (HR admin). Each view shared the same underlying data model but surfaced different insights and calls-to-action.

Iterating with HR stakeholders, we simplified the competency visualization from a complex radar chart to a tiered progress system that was immediately readable. Design system components were built to scale across all three permission levels.

04 — Outcome

A platform that bridges HR strategy and daily action

Role-Based Dashboards

Three distinct but connected views — employee, manager, HR admin — each surfacing the right data at the right level of detail.

Competency Visualization

Simplified complex leadership frameworks into readable progress indicators with contextual benchmarks and recommended next steps.

Design System

Built a modular component library covering data visualization, form patterns, and notification systems for scalable future feature development.
